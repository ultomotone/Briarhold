# BUILD DOCTRINE — how to run this over months without it going stale

## The increment

One per run. A mechanic, a system, a creature, an obstacle, interface work, information design,
feel, content, world state, a rebalance, performance, or a fix. Interface work with no new content
is a full increment.

## The roadmap era

While `docs/ROADMAP.md` has unchecked steps, the increment is the topmost unchecked step. The
roadmap fixes WHAT; the builder owns HOW. Steps marked `[owner]` are owner-present sessions and
are never attempted by a scheduled run.

## Weekly-visible rule

Every seven consecutive runs include at least one increment visible in the first thirty seconds
of play. If six runs have been plumbing, the seventh is something he can see from the Hold.

## Increment mix, over any two weeks

New system 3 · deepen existing 4 · interface and feel 2 · world event 2 · secret or discovery 2 ·
fix and rebalance 1. Not mechanical; a drift check. Four "new system" runs in a row means the
game is wide and thin.

## Visual threads pause the clock

When a visual or rendering thread is open in `07_WORKBENCH.md`, stages may be invisible for
several builds while a pipeline comes together. State the budget up front anyway. Two consecutive
builds producing nothing visible and nothing structural is a stalled thread: ship the smaller
version or kill it.

## The surprise budget

Once a week, ship something he could not have predicted from what already exists. The other days
are craft: deepen, sharpen, fix.

## Choosing when the roadmap is empty

0. Open workbench thread? Advance it one stage.
1. Anything broken or unreadable? Fix it.
2. What did he do most, and what is thinnest about it? Deepen that.
3. What has the world been threatening that hasn't happened? Make it happen.
4. Anything hard to read or slow to use? Interface work is a real increment.
5. A week without a surprise? This is the day.

## Silence

No verdict for three builds: stop shipping new systems, deepen what exists. He plays and says
nothing: treat the last thing you built as Watch, not Keep. Never nag for a verdict.
