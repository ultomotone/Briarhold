# MASTER INSTRUCTIONS — the contract

This repo builds one game, Briarhold, for one player, Daniel, one increment per scheduled run, with
no one watching. Everything else here serves that.

## The relationship

- He plays. He does not write specs, feature requests, or tickets. He may drop a verdict in chat
  or edit a doc; both are recorded in `06_VERDICTS.md` and bind from then on.
- The builder decides. Above the foundation (`08_FOUNDATION.md`) the builder has full authority
  over mechanics, content, names, tone, art direction, and pacing. Decide confidently, build,
  log the reasoning. Never ask him what to build.
- The roadmap is open (`docs/ROADMAP.md`); he can read what is coming. The execution is the
  surprise: how a step lands, what it looks like, what it is called.

## This is not a business project

Ignore every global preference about revenue, cash flow, premortems, strategic challenge, phase
discipline, and OpsPal. Do not reference his work, finances, health, relationships, personality
type, or any psychometrics. Do not build a character, scenario, or mechanic derived from any of
it. If it feels like the game is analysing him, you have failed. His own framing: this is
nights-and-weekends regulation and play. The scheduled builder is the time-box; it does the
hours so he does not have to.

## The three laws of the design

1. **Randomness sets the problem, skill solves it.** Random contents, never random outcomes.
   Where a grub sleeps is rolled; whether its bite lands is not. No hit chance, no damage rolls,
   no crit stat, no dice anywhere in combat.
2. **Matchup prices, investment decides.** A type or class advantage sets a price, never an
   outcome. Enough invested advantage overcomes any matchup. A disadvantage is a tax the strong
   can afford and the weak cannot.
3. **Nothing is ever lost.** Swarm sprigs are expected losses. Wardens wilt to seed and come
   back. The Hold total only rises. Banked things are safe.

## Quality bar

- Playable after every run. A working old build beats a broken new one.
- Readable in the first thirty seconds of play. Every seven runs, at least one increment is
  visible before he touches a menu.
- Interface plain, content named. See the naming law in `06_VERDICTS.md`.
- Dry, competent, unsentimental register. No exclamation marks in anything he reads.
- Minimal text in the game. Situations, not dialogue. No story he must sit through.

## Scope discipline

- One increment per run. Stage multi-run work in `07_WORKBENCH.md` with a finish condition and
  a build budget.
- Never generate work for him. A review document written for him is written in plain words,
  one line per item, with a stated way to answer in a sentence.
- Chassis replacement is never the builder's call. Engine, renderer, language, and the sim/view
  split change only through an owner-present decision recorded in `06_VERDICTS.md`.
