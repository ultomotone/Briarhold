Every sprig type now has a territory of its own on a ring around the Hold: the Mere (Tide), the Wire Fence (Spark), the Fume Bog (Thorn), the Stone Stacks (Moth), the Frost Hollow (Frost), and the Fallen Oak (Oak), each with its shrine, sprouts, and a cache only that type can bring home; the minimap shows them as "?" until you walk in. Try first: head for the nearest "?" on the minimap, pluck the four sprouts by its shrine, and send that type after the relic.
