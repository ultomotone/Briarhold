The world is an open one now: 500 m with no pattern to it, green country round the Hold turning to snow one way and sand or jungle another, woods with hollow logs to walk through, tipped flower pots, ruins, five campsites you can move the Hold to (hold T at a beacon), and three bosses in arenas; the sprigs have their long stems and big eyes. Try first: zoom right out (wheel), pick a direction on the map, walk until the banner says you have crossed into snow, sand or jungle, and find a camp there.
