BRIARHOLD

Windows
  1. Unzip Briarhold-windows.zip anywhere.
  2. Double-click Briarhold.exe.
     Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't signed.
     Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Linux (64-bit, needs a graphics driver with Vulkan)
  1. Unpack:            tar -xzf Briarhold-linux.tar.gz
  2. Run:               ./Briarhold.x86_64
     If it says permission denied, first run:  chmod +x Briarhold.x86_64

On the title screen pick New Hold.

Controls
  WASD          move the gnome
  Right-click   walk to a spot
  Left-click    sword
  F             pluck sprouts; at a shrine, take sprigs out
  G             at a shrine, send sprigs in
  Space         throw (hold to keep throwing)
  R             whistle them back
  X             dismiss    C  charge    Shift  swarm
  Q / E         pick sprig type
  Mouse wheel   zoom
  Middle mouse  hold and drag to turn the camera
  Z / V         turn the camera
  T             at a camp's beacon (hold): move the Hold there
  Esc           pause

Get home to the ring before night. Things come out after dark.
The world is big: snow one way, sand or jungle another. Snow slows all but Frost sprigs,
sand all but Embers, jungle all but Thorns. Find a camp out there and move the Hold to it.
The game saves on its own, in your user folder.
