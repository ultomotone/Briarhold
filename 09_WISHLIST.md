# WISHLIST — things he has said he wants

A pool, not a queue. Drawn from when the roadmap is empty or the surprise budget is due.
Source: the player profile and the commissioning conversation.

- More creature variety than Pikmin. Hybrids. Types as data so the roster keeps growing.
- Gadgets. A workshop at the Hold, bought with relic value.
- Collecting for a complete set. Relic sets with completion bonuses. A codex that fills in.
- Skill trees and attribute buildouts for him; class evolution for the named ones.
- Delegation: "go do this until it's done." Work-crews that keep working while he is away.
- Visible damage math, Wild Rift style. Every number on screen with its cause.
- A world that keeps expanding. Secrets he might never find. A map that fills in.
- Caves where you never know what is in them (Morrowind). Burrows that re-roll.
- Fast travel that is fine because the destination is worth it.
- A score he can beat. A daily challenge. Ranking up, in a single-player world.
- A village: sprigs building it, working around it on their own, and defending it at night
  against marauders and animals that can go anywhere on the map. (2026-09-02, in play.)
- Right-click access to sprigs held inside the shrine. (Done: hold F at a shrine.)
- Territory he can lose. Something happening in a place: a boss, dense resources.
- An economy worth accumulating in. Serious wealth. A bank of hundreds of things held for
  specific parts of the game.
- Retreating as a real option. Losing to his own bad strategy is fine; being obliterated is not.
- Time invested leaves a visible mark.
